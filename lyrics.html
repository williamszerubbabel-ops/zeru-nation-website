Samehe Sabini Mara Saba

Zeru Nation Worship

[Verse 1]

Moyo wangu ulikuwa mzito,
Umebeba uchungu na majeraha.
Lakini sauti yako inasema,
Acha yote, mwachie Bwana.

[Chorus]

Samehe sabini mara saba,
Fungua moyo wako uwe huru.
Upendo wa Yesu hauna mipaka,
Samehe wenzako upate amani.

[Verse 2]

Haustahili kuishi na amani tu,
Pale unapoachilia msamaha.
Giza linakimbia mwangaza ukija,
Bwana Yesu anaponya roho yako.

[Bridge]

Msamaha ni nguvu, msamaha ni uzima,
Katika Jina la Yesu, tuko huru leo!

Scripture Inspiration

"Petro akamwendea akamwambia, 'Bwana, ndugu yangu anikosie mara ngapi nami nimsamehe? Hata mara saba?' Yesu akamwambia, 'Sikuambii hata mara saba, bali hata sabini mara saba.'"

Mathayo 18:21-22

Story Behind the Song

This worship piece was birthed during a prayer retreat when the ministry reflected on the weight of unforgiveness holding back believers from experiencing God's full glory. It serves as an anthem calling nations to release past pains and embrace Christ's unlimited grace.
